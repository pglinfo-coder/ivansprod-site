I first saw Explee in a YouTube ad. The idea caught my attention: find potential clients and send them a presentation email, for a small cost per email. I found it on my own, searching the web. Instead of trusting the promises, I tested it on my daughter’s website.

## What I did

I created a project and gave it her website as a reference. Explee searched for potential clients and built six email campaigns, each aimed at a different type of customer. In total I sent 980 emails. You pay as you go, with no subscription. A $30 free credit came with the account, and my credit card was charged $5 on top of it. Each email cost about $0.04, and I spent $39.20 in all.

## What worked

The replies came in fast. About 6.2% of the emails got an answer, and Explee flagged 32 of those replies as “hot leads”, which works out to about $1.23 per lead. Its best campaign, aimed at senior dog caregivers, got a 9.7% reply rate at $0.68 per lead.

Note that “hot lead” is Explee’s own label for a reply that sounds interested. It is not a paying customer.

## What to expect as a beginner

After about an hour of setup, everything was ready for the first campaign. Explee does the searching and the writing, but it doesn’t decide for you who to target. The settings you choose are what make the difference between useful replies and wasted money. If you’re on your own and have never learned software of this kind, expect to spend time understanding it before your first send.

## What didn’t go so well

My daughter’s business is local, in Laval, Quebec, and her website is in French. In the targeting setting, I chose all of Canada instead of picking individual cities close by. I only noticed my mistake when I read the replies from across the country. One person in British Columbia told me the site was in French and that she could translate it. The emails were reaching people who could never become her customers.

I stopped the mass sending as soon as I understood. Then I took the time to learn how the tool really works.

I also made a second mistake, this time with follow-up. I got two callback requests by phone, and neither my daughter nor I followed up. I had run the test for her without telling her first, so she wasn’t expecting the calls. I believe that with proper follow-up I could have turned perhaps four replies into real contacts.

To avoid my mistakes:

1. Before sending, choose the cities close to your business one by one. Don’t choose a whole country.
2. Make sure the person who will receive the replies knows the test is happening.
3. Plan time to answer every interested reply within a day or so.

## Making it fit the way you work

Used with the right settings, Explee is a good tool for finding new clients, and the cost per contact is reasonable. It suits a solo business owner with a local or clearly defined service, who is ready to learn the settings and to follow up every promising reply. It is not a good fit for someone working alone with no experience of this kind of software, or for anyone who won’t have the time to answer.
